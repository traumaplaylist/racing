CANYON CLASH v0.1 (Windows x86-64, offline)

Run: unzip anywhere and start CanyonClash.exe. No installer, no network.
Needs a GPU with OpenGL 3.3 (Godot Compatibility renderer).
Progress is saved to %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Canyon Clash\profile.json
(plus profile.bak.json as the last valid backup).

CONTROLS            Keyboard            Xbox gamepad
Throttle            W / Up              RT
Brake / reverse     S / Down            LT
Steer               A D / Left Right    Left stick
Handbrake (drift)   Space               A
Fire                J                   RB
Reset car           R                   Y
Pause               Esc                 Menu
Menus: mouse, arrows/D-pad/left stick, Enter/A select, Esc/B back.
F11 fullscreen, F3 FPS counter.

Win by crossing the line first after 3 laps, or by being the last car running.
